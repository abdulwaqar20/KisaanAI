# --- COTTON DISEASES ---
# CROP: COTTON | DISEASE: Target Spot
Cotton Target Spot disease features prominent concentric target-like rings with brown margins on leaf tissue. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Pyraclostrobin + Fluxapyroxad (Brand Example: Priaxor 300SC by BASF Pakistan). Dosage: 200 ml per acre dissolved in 100 liters of clean water. Price Note: Premium tier systemic control for cotton target spot.

# CROP: COTTON | DISEASE: Ascochyta Blight
Cotton Ascochyta Blight disease displays ash-grey lesions wrapped in very distinctive dark purplish outer borders on young cotton leaves. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Thiophanate-Methyl (Brand Example: Topsin-M 70WP by Japan Agro Pakistan). Dosage: 400 grams per acre. Price Note: Highly affordable broad-spectrum curative treatment for cotton blight.

# CROP: COTTON | DISEASE: Bacterial Leaf Blight
Cotton Bacterial Leaf Blight disease manifests as water-soaked angular spots on leaves that turn dark brown or black over time. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Copper Oxychloride + Kasugamycin (Brand Example: Kanub 50WP). Dosage: 250 grams per acre. Price Note: Effective bacterial shield for cotton fields.

# CROP: COTTON | DISEASE: Leaf Curl Virus
Cotton Leaf Curl Virus disease causes upward or downward curling of cotton leaves with thickening of veins and small leaf-like enations on the underside. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Thiamethoxam (Brand Example: Actara 25WG by Syngenta Pakistan). Dosage: 24 grams per acre to eliminate whitefly vectors. Price Note: Standard premium whitefly vector mitigation for cotton crops.

# --- RICE DISEASES ---
# CROP: RICE | DISEASE: Rice Blast
Rice Blast disease forms spindle-shaped or diamond-shaped lesions with grayish centers and dark reddish borders on rice crop leaves. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Tricyclazole (Brand Example: Beam 75WP by Dow AgroSciences). Dosage: 120 grams per acre. Price Note: Economical protective fungicide for rice blast control.

# CROP: RICE | DISEASE: Brown Leaf Spot
Rice Brown Leaf Spot disease produces small, oval, relatively uniform dark brown lesions evenly distributed across the rice plant leaf blades. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Mancozeb (Brand Example: Dithane M-45). Dosage: 500 grams per acre. Price Note: Affordable traditional multi-site protective choice for rice crop varieties.

# CROP: RICE | DISEASE: Stem Rot
Rice Stem Rot disease triggers irregular black lesions on outer leaf sheaths near the water line, causing the rice stalks to rot and collapse. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Hexaconazole (Brand Example: Anvil 5SC). Dosage: 400 ml per acre. Price Note: Systemic azole solution targeting rice stem fungal decay.

# --- WHEAT DISEASES ---
# CROP: WHEAT | DISEASE: Leaf Rust
Wheat Leaf Rust disease shows bright orange-brown pustules scattering across the wheat plant canopy during spring humid windows. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Tebuconazole (Brand Example: Folicur 250EC by Bayer CropScience Pakistan). Dosage: 200 ml per acre. Price Note: Trusted broad-spectrum curative treatment for wheat leaf rust.

# CROP: WHEAT | DISEASE: Yellow Rust
Wheat Yellow Rust disease forms distinct linear stripes of bright yellow pustules along the veins of wheat plant foliage during cool windows. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Propiconazole (Brand Example: Tilt 250EC by Syngenta Pakistan). Dosage: 200 ml per acre. Price Note: High-performing systemic cure for wheat stripe rust.

# CROP: WHEAT | DISEASE: Powdery Mildew
Wheat Powdery Mildew disease displays patches of white, fluffy, powdery fungal growth covering the upper surface of wheat leaf tissue. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Flutriafol (Brand Example: Impact 125SC). Dosage: 200 ml per acre. Price Note: Broad-spectrum protective triazole targeting wheat mildew cycles.

# --- TOMATO DISEASES ---
# CROP: TOMATO | DISEASE: Early Blight
Tomato Early Blight disease creates dark brown leathery spots with distinct target-board concentric ridges starting on older tomato plant foliage. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Copper Oxychloride (Brand Example: Cobox 50WP). Dosage: 500 grams per acre. Price Note: Cost-effective traditional protective organic copper agent for tomato blight.

# CROP: TOMATO | DISEASE: Late Blight
Tomato Late Blight disease causes large, irregular water-soaked dark lesions on tomato leaves that quickly turn brown and rot under wet weather conditions. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Cymoxanil + Mancozeb (Brand Example: Curzate M8 by Corteva Pakistan). Dosage: 250 grams per acre. Price Note: Rapid penetration preventive mixture for tomato late blight.

# CROP: TOMATO | DISEASE: Tomato Leaf Curl
Tomato Tomato Leaf Curl disease displays severe stunting of the tomato plant along with yellowing and dramatic upward rolling of young leaf margins. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Acetamiprid (Brand Example: Mospilan 20SP). Dosage: 150 grams per acre to destroy whitefly vectors. Price Note: Effective vector system response for tomato leaf protection.

# --- CITRUS DISEASES ---
# CROP: CITRUS | DISEASE: Citrus Canker
Citrus Citrus Canker disease displays raised, corky, rough brown lesions on leaves and fruits, always wrapped in a bright yellow-chlorotic halo. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Copper Hydroxide (Brand Example: Kocide 2000DF by Certis Pakistan). Dosage: 250 grams per acre dissolved in 100 liters of water. Price Note: Mid-tier standard premium protective copper shield for citrus canker.

# CROP: CITRUS | DISEASE: Citrus Scab
Citrus Citrus Scab disease produces corky, raised pustules or scab lesions on leaves, twigs, and fruit which become tan to grey over time. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Benomyl (Brand Example: Benlate 50WP). Dosage: 200 grams per acre. Price Note: Systemic fungicide targeting structural scab cells in citrus.

# --- MANGO DISEASES ---
# CROP: MANGO | DISEASE: Mango Anthracnose
Mango Mango Anthracnose disease manifests as irregular, dark brown to black necrotic tear-stain spots spreading along the mango leaf margins and fruits. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Azoxystrobin + Difenoconazole (Brand Example: Amistar Top by Syngenta Pakistan). Dosage: 200 ml per acre. Price Note: Highly effective systemic curative option for mango anthracnose.

# CROP: MANGO | DISEASE: Mango Powdery Mildew
Mango Mango Powdery Mildew disease displays a powdery whitish fungal growth on leaves, flowers, and young fruits causing severe blossom drop. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Dinocap (Brand Example: Karathane). Dosage: 150 ml per acre dissolved in water. Price Note: Specific target curative control for mango powdery mildew outbreaks.

# --- OTHER REGIONAL CASH CROPS ---
# CROP: SUGARCANE | DISEASE: Red Rot
Sugarcane Red Rot disease triggers severe internal reddening of the pith with distinctive white patches along the stalk internodes. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Thiophanate-Methyl (Brand Example: Topsin-M 70WP by Japan Agro Pakistan). Dosage: 400 grams per acre dissolved in clean water. Price Note: Safe and approved broad-spectrum systemic fungicide for sugarcane red rot control.

# CROP: POTATO | DISEASE: Black Scurf
Potato Black Scurf disease forms hard, black or dark brown irregular fungal crusts (sclerotia) that tightly stick to the potato tuber surfaces. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Flutolanil (Brand Example: Moncut 25WP). Dosage: 200 grams per acre applied at seed piece treatment phase. Price Note: Targeted protection against potato black scurf fungal strains.

# CROP: CHICKPEA | DISEASE: Ascochyta blight
Chickpea Ascochyta blight produces brown circular lesions with dark borders and concentric rings on leaves and pods. Approved Chemical Control Strategy: Active Ingredient: Chlorothalonil or Mancozeb (Brand Example: Bravo 720 / Dithane M-45). Dosage: 500 grams per acre. Price Note: Effective foliar protective fungicide for chickpea blight.
